Easily integrate MultiSafepay payment solutions into your PrestaShop webshop with our free plugin.

Before you get started, read MultiSafepay Docs - [PrestaShop](https://docs.multisafepay.com/docs/prestashop/) for:

- Supported payment methods
- Installation and configuration
- Plugin updates and troubleshooting

## About MultiSafepay

MultiSafepay is a collecting payment service provider, which means we take care of electronic contracts, technical details, and payment collection for each payment method. You can start selling online today, and manage all your transactions in one place.

## Supported payment methods

See MultiSafepay Docs – [PrestaShop](https://docs.multisafepay.com/docs/prestashop/).

## Prerequisites

- You will need a [MultiSafepay account](https://testmerchant.multisafepay.com/signup). Consider a test account first.
- PrestaShop 1.7.6 or higher (including PrestaShop 8 and 9)
- PHP version compatible with your PrestaShop version

## Installation

1. Download the latest plugin release from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/MultiSafepay/prestashop-official/releases).
2. Sign in to your PrestaShop backend.
3. Go to **Modules** > **Module manager** > **Upload a module**.
4. Select the `Plugin_PrestaShop_x.x.x.zip` file, and then click **Configure**.
5. Clear your cache.

## How to configure

1. Sign in to your PrestaShop backend.
2. Go to **Modules** > **Module manager** > **Upload a module**.
3. For **MultiSafepay**, click **Configure**.
4. Enter your [API key](https://docs.multisafepay.com/docs/sites#site-id-api-key-and-security-code) and save.
5. Enable the payment methods you want to offer.
6. Confirm your preferred order and payment settings.

For more information, see MultiSafepay Docs – [PrestaShop](https://docs.multisafepay.com/docs/prestashop/).
